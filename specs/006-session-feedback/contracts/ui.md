# Contract: screens

## Today

- When there's a kept plan that isn't used up, a card above "What kind of session?" shows:
  "Your planned session", routine name and length (e.g. "Calm morning · 15 min"), "Planned
  {day}" when not planned today, and any repair notes. Buttons: **Review** (plan screen) and
  **Start** (player). Only the most recently viewed such plan is shown.
- "Plan my {n}-minute {routine}" opens the plan screen for that routine and length; it shows the
  kept plan if there is one.

## Plan screen

- Shows the kept plan (or makes and keeps a new one). A repair shows its notes above the list,
  e.g. "Child’s pose is now Not for me, so it was swapped for Puppy pose."
- **Swap** on an item and **Reshuffle** are buttons that change the kept plan (no longer links
  that add URL counters). After Reshuffle the screen shows the new plan.
- Planned on an earlier day: "Planned {day}" under the title.

## Player and exercise page

- Next to the technique tag: a pace tag in text, one of **Hold**, **Slow and controlled**,
  **Flowing**, **Brisk**.
- The figure plays at that pace (research R6).

## Rating sheet

| Button | Hint |
|---|---|
| Too easy | — |
| Just right | — |
| Too hard | — |
| Skip next time | Not for me: won’t be suggested again |

After Skip next time, the next screen shows for 6 s (or until dismissed): "{name} won’t be
suggested again. Undo from its page in the Library."

## Finish screen

"Ratings are saved; you’ll see them in Progress." replaces the line about the planner.
